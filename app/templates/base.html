Terms of Service — PARTBASE
1. Zero Tolerance for Job Fees

PARTBASE strictly prohibits employers or anyone posting jobs from requesting any registration fees, security deposits, kit costs, or advance payments. Any listing asking for payment is terminated immediately and reported.

2. Hyper-Local Platform

PARTBASE connects neighborhood businesses in Chennai (OMR, Anna Nagar) with nearby seekers for micro-jobs.

3. Safety & Direct Engagement

Payments for work done are settled directly between employer and seeker. PARTBASE does not collect commissions from seekers.

Privacy Policy — PARTBASE
1. Password-Free Mobile Authentication

We use mobile numbers and one-time passwords (OTP) only. We do not store passwords or require email addresses.

2. Location & Privacy

Your exact GPS coordinates are never made public. Only your selected area and approximate distance (e.g. 1.2 km) are shared with employers when you apply.

3. Data Protection

Phone numbers are masked across public pages and revealed only upon mutual hiring.
